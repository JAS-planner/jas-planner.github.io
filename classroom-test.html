Google Classroom connection test

Checks whether JAS can read your Google Classroom. It only reads: your classes, assignments with due dates, announcements and materials. Nothing is saved, and nothing is posted to Classroom. The results stay in this browser tab.

1. Google Client ID

Paste the Client ID from Google Cloud. It ends in .apps.googleusercontent.com. It is not a secret.

2. Connect

Sign in with the Google account you use for Classroom. If Google shows "Google hasn't verified this app", press Continue. That is normal while JAS is in testing.
