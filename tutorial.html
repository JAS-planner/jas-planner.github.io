Part 1

Getting started

01

Create an account

  1. Press Sign up at the top of the form.
  2. Enter your name, email and a password (at least 8 characters).
  3. Tick the Terms box, then press Create account.

Already have an account? Use the Sign in tab. Tick “Keep me signed in” so you don't have to sign in again.

02

The sidebar on the left

  1. Pages: About JAS (Page 1), Boards (Page 2), Feedback & Reviews (Page 3).
  2. Modes: College Recon for your Google Classroom deadlines, and the AI connector.
  3. At the bottom are your profile card and Log out.

On a phone, press the ☰ button at the top to open the sidebar.

03

Create a board

  1. Go to Boards in the sidebar.
  2. Press + Create board.
  3. A blank board opens. Click the name at the top to rename it, for example “This week’s plan”.

Saving is automatic. You will see “Saving…” and then “Saved” at the top of the board.

Part 2

On the board

04

Sticky notes and text

  1. Press the Sticky note tool (N), then click anywhere on the board.
  2. For a heading, use the Text tool (T).
  3. Type your text, then press Esc when you are done.

Faster: double-click an empty spot to add a sticky note. Double-click a note to edit it again.

05

Colour, size and labels

  1. Click a note. A toolbar appears above it.
  2. Pick a colour, or press the colour wheel for any colour you like.
  3. The same toolbar has text size (S, M, L), labels, Lock, duplicate and delete.
06

Shapes for diagrams

  1. Press the Shape tool (S).
  2. Pick from the menu: rectangle, circle, diamond, start/end, database and more.
  3. Click on the board to place it, then type its text.
07

Connect with arrows

  1. Press the Connector tool (C).
  2. Click the first note, then click the second one. An arrow now joins them.
  3. When you move a note, the arrow follows.

You can also drag the green dot on the side of a selected note onto another note.

08

Select many and group

  1. Press the Select many tool (M).
  2. Drag a box around the notes you want. Everything inside gets selected.
  3. Drag one of them to move them all together. Press Group (Ctrl+G) to keep them together.
09

Tables and charts for statistics

  1. Press the Diagram, table and chart button.
  2. Pick one: Flowchart, Table, Bar chart, Line chart, Pie chart or Statistics.
  3. Double-click a table cell to change a number. The chart updates by itself.

In a table, Enter moves down to the next row and Tab moves right.

10

Drawing, highlighter and eraser

  1. Press the Draw tool (P).
  2. Pick Pen, Highlighter or Eraser (E), then a colour and thickness.
  3. Draw anywhere. To erase, drag the Eraser over a stroke.
11

Board background colour

  1. Press the Board background button.
  2. Pick white, cream, green, black or another colour.
  3. Use the colour wheel for your own colour.
12

Lock notes so nobody moves them

  1. Select a note (or a group of notes).
  2. Press Lock in the toolbar, or Ctrl+L.
  3. The note now shows a lock icon. Nobody can move, edit or delete it until someone presses Unlock.
Part 3

Working with others

13

Share a board

  1. On the board, press Share at the top.
  2. Enter the JAS email of the person you want to add and pick Can edit (editor) or Can view (viewer only).
  3. Press Add. The board now shows up on their Boards page.

Only the owner can add people or change their access. You can also set a different name for yourself on this board.

14

Work live and follow a teammate

  1. When you are on a board together, you see their picture at the top, their pointer, and what they are moving.
  2. Click their picture to follow their view. Wherever they look, your screen goes too.
  3. Press Stop to stop following.
Part 4

Your account and more

15

Your profile

  1. Click the profile card at the bottom of the sidebar.
  2. Change your picture, name and bio.
  3. Press Save profile. People you share boards with see it in the Share list.
16

Feedback and reviews

  1. Go to Feedback & Reviews in the sidebar.
  2. Pick Suggestion, Feedback or Review. For a review, pick the stars.
  3. Type your message and press Send.

Reviews are shown to everyone. Suggestions and feedback go to the JAS team only.

17

On a phone

  1. The tools sit at the bottom of the screen. Swipe left to see more.
  2. Pinch to zoom, and drag an empty spot to move around.
  3. Press Fit to see the whole board.
